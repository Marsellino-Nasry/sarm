Compliance & GRC

A clear, evidenced path to ISO/IEC 27001 certification and the regulations that apply to the business.

  • PCI DSS readiness and NIST CSF maturity

Offensive Security

Authorized, hands-on testing that shows what an attacker could actually reach, and how to stop them.

  • Web application and API penetration testing (OWASP)

  • Red team and adversary emulation (MITRE ATT&CK), wireless and OSINT

Signature engagement

The Dual-Lens Assessment

Most providers send an auditor or a penetration tester. Here you get both in one person. Every exploitable weakness is traced to the control that should have stopped it, mapped to ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT and your regulator's requirements, and fixed at the root.

How an engagement works

  1. 01

    Scope

    Free 30-minute call, then a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and fee. NDA before sensitive detail is shared.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Evaluate control design against ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT, COSO or the regulator's framework. Gaps ranked by risk.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Operating-effectiveness testing, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing under signed rules of engagement.

  4. 04

    Assure

    Executive briefing, technical walkthrough, agreed remediation with control owners, and a retest to confirm fixes hold.

  • Confidential by default: NDA as standard
  • Authorized testing only
  • Business-language reporting
  • Fixes verified by retest

Engagement models

  • Fixed-scope assessment

    One defined project and deliverable: an ITGC audit, an ISO/IEC 27001 gap assessment or a penetration test.

  • Readiness programme

    From gap assessment to audit or certification readiness over several months, with milestones and progress reporting.

  • Advisory retainer

    Ongoing control reviews, policy updates, vendor security reviews, periodic vulnerability assessments and audit support.
