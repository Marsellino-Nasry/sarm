Approach

How an engagement works

A predictable process with no surprises. The three arrows in the SARM mark stand for its core stages: Assess, Validate, Assure.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A free 30-minute call to understand your goals, deadline and environment. You then receive a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and fee. NDA signed before any sensitive detail is shared.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Understand the business and evaluate control design against ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT, COSO or your regulator's framework. The result is a gap view ranked by risk.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Test how controls perform in practice: operating-effectiveness sampling, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing under signed rules of engagement.

  4. 04

    Assure

    An executive briefing for management and a technical walkthrough for IT. We agree remediation with control owners, and I retest to confirm the fixes hold.

  • Confidential by default. NDA as standard; client names and findings are never published.
  • Authorized testing only. Written authorization and rules of engagement before any test.
  • Business language. Risk explained in terms of money, operations and regulation.
  • Fixes verified. Engagements end with confirmed remediation, not just a report.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first

Don't see yours? Ask it in the form below. A straight answer costs nothing.

Can you certify us for ISO/IEC 27001?

No consultant should. Certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies, and independence rules stop the person who helps you implement from certifying you. What I do is get you ready: gap assessment, ISMS build, internal audit and Stage 1 and Stage 2 preparation, with support while the certification audit runs.

Is penetration testing safe for our live systems?

Testing only starts after written authorization and agreed rules of engagement: scope, testing windows, emergency contacts and any systems that are off-limits. Higher-risk tests can be scheduled out of hours or run against a staging environment.

How do you handle our confidential information?

I sign an NDA before you share sensitive detail, collect only the evidence the engagement needs, and agree with you how it is returned or deleted at the end. Client names and findings are never published or used in marketing.

How much does an engagement cost?

It depends on scope: the number of systems, applications, locations and frameworks involved. After a free scoping call you receive a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and fee, so you know the full cost before any work begins.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. Most audit and compliance work runs well remotely. Internal network and Active Directory testing can be done on-site or over a secure connection you provide.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short call and a named contact. For audits and compliance: your policies, a system inventory and the people who own the evidence. For testing: a signed authorization and the list of targets in scope.
